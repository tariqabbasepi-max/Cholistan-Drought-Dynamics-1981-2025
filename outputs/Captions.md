# Figure and table captions (generated from the analysis)


## Main figures

**Fig01_study_area.** Cholistan Desert study area (boundary area 24,790 km²) with the native CHIRPS 0.05° grid; shaded cells (n = 915) are ≥50% inside the boundary and form the spatial analysis domain. OSM layers not available; boundary and CHIRPS grid only.

**Fig02_rainfall_regime.** Rainfall regime of the Cholistan Desert from area-weighted CHIRPS v2 pentads. (a) Monthly climatology 1991–2020 (bars: mean; whiskers: ±1 SD); JJAS supplies 80% of the annual mean. (b) Annual and JJAS totals, 1981–2025; dashed line: 1991–2020 annual mean.

**Fig03_SPI_3_6_12.** Standardized Precipitation Index at (a) 3, (b) 6 and (c) 12 months, 1981–2025 (gamma distribution, L-moment estimation by ending calendar month, full-record calibration). Purple: SPI < 0. Dashed: moderate drought threshold (−1); dotted: severe (−1.5) and extreme (−2).

**Fig04_shift_versus_trend.** Abrupt shift versus gradual trend in annual rainfall. (a) Annual totals with segment means, the 1981–2025 Sen trend and the best-BIC hinge model (break 2004); shading: 95% block-bootstrap range of the Pettitt change year (2002–2010). (b) Bootstrap distribution of the change year (2000 replicates). (c) BIC weights of five competing models. The Pettitt test rejected in 96% of series simulated from a pure linear trend, so it cannot by itself distinguish a step from a trend.

**Fig05_wet_pentad_frequency_intensity.** Source of the rainfall increase. (a) Annual rainfall split into pentads <5 mm and ≥5 mm (CHIRPS v2). (b) Relative Sen trends with 95% block-bootstrap CIs of rain in wet pentads, their number (frequency) and mean amount (intensity). Rain in wet pentads increased by 2.94 mm yr⁻¹ of the 3.05 mm yr⁻¹ annual trend.

**Fig06_pixel_trend_maps.** Pixel-wise Sen slopes (per decade), CHIRPS v2, 1981–2025, for (a) annual rainfall, (b) JJAS rainfall, (c) number of wet pentads (≥5 mm) and (d) annual minimum SPI-12 (1982–2025). Dots: cells significant after BH-FDR at q ≤ 0.10 (Hamed–Rao MK); area significant: 100%, 100%, 99%, 100%. Green: wetter/less severe.

**Fig07_LISA_trend_clusters.** Local Moran's I clusters of the trend fields in Fig. 6 (queen contiguity, 9999 conditional permutations, BH-FDR q ≤ 0.05). High–High (Low–Low): cells whose slope and neighbourhood slope both exceed (fall below) the Cholistan mean slope; they describe relative strength of change, not its sign.

**Fig08_precipitation_product_agreement.** Consistency of the rainfall signal across precipitation products (area-weighted Cholistan means). (a) Annual totals as % of each product's 2001–2020 mean; dotted line: 2009/2010. (b) Change of the mean after 2009 relative to the earlier period (IMERG from 2001, PERSIANN-CDR from 1983). Driest year in all products: 2002.

(b) Near-rainless P20 dry pentads in CHIRPS v2 and v3; homogeneity tests place a break in v2 at 2000. Dots: BH-FDR q ≤ 0.10. Brown: drier tendency.

**FigS3_CHIRPS_v2_v3_trend_maps.** CHIRPS v2 (a, c) versus CHIRPS v3 (b, d) Sen slopes per decade on the same 0.05° grid: annual rainfall (a, b) and P20 dry pentads counted over all calendar pentads (c, d). Dots: BH-FDR q ≤ 0.10.

**FigS4_exploratory_rainfall_zones.** Exploratory rainfall zones. (a) 2 contiguous zones from spatially constrained Ward clustering of standardized annual-rainfall anomalies (silhouette 0.27; year-resampling ARI 0.57). (b) Area-weighted annual rainfall per zone.


## Main tables

**Table_1_major_meteorological_drought_episodes.** Major meteorological drought episodes, 1981–2025. Strict runs (consecutive months with SPI ≤ −1) at SPI-3, -6 and -12 with peak ≤ −1.5 or duration ≥ 6 months were merged across scales when separated by ≤ 62 days; episodes involving ≥ 2 scales, peaking ≤ −2, or lasting ≥ 6 months are listed. SPI: gamma, L-moments, full-record calibration.

**Table_2_participant_derived_resilience_priorities.** Resilience priorities reported by participants in the consultations (descriptive synthesis; not measured livestock, forage or livelihood outcomes). 'Greater' and 'Lesser' Cholistan are participants' own terms.

**Table_3_trend_robustness_CHIRPS_v2_v3.** Regional trends (Sen slope per decade with 95% block-bootstrap CI and Hamed–Rao p) and the difference between 2010–2025 and 1981–2009 means for key metrics in CHIRPS v2 and v3. 'Robust' requires p < 0.05 with the same sign in both versions.

**Table_4_precipitation_product_agreement.** Agreement of CHIRPS v2 with CHIRPS v3, PERSIANN-CDR, ERA5-Land and IMERG V07 over each product's overlap (area-weighted regional means): correlation, Kling–Gupta efficiency and bias relative to CHIRPS v2, Sen trend (Hamed–Rao p), change of the mean after 2009, Pettitt year, driest year and SPI-12 agreement. Agreement does not imply accuracy; no gauge data were available.


## Supplementary tables

**Table_S01_reproduction_of_reported_values.** Values reported in the submitted manuscript re-derived from the archived regional CHIRPS v2 series.

**Table_S02_monthly_climatology_1991_2020.** Monthly rainfall climatology, 1991–2020 (area-weighted CHIRPS v2).

**Table_S03_annual_and_monsoon_rainfall.** Annual, JJAS and JAS rainfall totals and annual departure from the 1991–2020 mean.

**Table_S04_SPI_gamma_parameters.** Gamma (L-moment) parameters and zero probability by SPI scale and ending calendar month; full-record calibration.

**Table_S05_SPI_goodness_of_fit.** Goodness of fit of the gamma model: Kolmogorov–Smirnov and Anderson–Darling with parametric-bootstrap p-values (parameters re-estimated in 999 replicates), Shapiro–Wilk of SPI; BH-adjusted across the 12 months of each scale.

**Table_S06_SPI_calibration_sensitivity.** SPI sensitivity to the calibration period and to the treatment of zero accumulations.

**Table_S07_SPI_event_summary.** Drought-month frequency, event counts and extremes by SPI scale.

**Table_S08_SPI_strict_threshold_runs.** All runs of consecutive months with SPI ≤ −1, by scale.

**Table_S09_SPI_event_envelopes.** Event envelopes: negative-SPI sequences that reach SPI ≤ −1 (onset at first SPI < 0, end before SPI ≥ 0).

**Table_S10_SPI_timeseries.** Monthly SPI-1, -3, -6 and -12 (primary formulation).

**Table_S11_trends_with_autocorrelation_diagnostics.** Hamed–Rao modified Mann–Kendall tests with Sen slopes (95% moving-block bootstrap CI, block length n^(1/3)), lag-1 autocorrelation of the detrended series, variance factor n/n*, effective sample size n*, unadjusted MK and trend-free pre-whitening p-values; BH q within family. For drought-month counts Sen slopes are degenerate (mostly zero); use Kendall's tau and p.

**Table_S12_changepoint_tests.** Pettitt, Buishand range (Q, R) and SNHT tests with permutation p-values (9,999 permutations); the change year is the last year of the first segment.

**Table_S13_step_versus_trend_models.** Annual rainfall: constant, linear-trend, step, step + trend and hinge-trend models compared by BIC and AICc (break year estimated by least squares and counted as a parameter).

**Table_S14_changepoint_uncertainty.** Block-bootstrap distribution of the Pettitt change year under the fitted step model, and the rejection rate of the Pettitt test when data are generated by a pure linear trend.

**Table_S15_subperiod_trends.** Trend sensitivity to the analysis window and after removing the mean shift at the candidate change year.

**Table_S16_segment_shift.** Segment means and Hodges–Lehmann shift (2010–2025 minus 1981–2009) with permutation p-values.

**Table_S17_dry_pentad_definition_sensitivity.** Trends in dry-pentad counts and longest runs under percentile definitions (calendar-pentad mid-rank percentile against 1991–2020; all pentads or only pentads with a 1991–2020 mean ≥1 or ≥2 mm) and absolute thresholds (<1, <2 mm). Ineligible pentads are not dry and break runs.

**Table_S18_P20_dry_pentads_by_regime.** Annual P20 dry pentads split into near-rainless calendar pentads (1991–2020 mean < 1 mm) and rain-bearing pentads.

**Table_S19_P20_attribution_and_homogeneity.** Trends of the two P20 components and homogeneity tests (Pettitt, Buishand, SNHT; permutation p) of the near-rainless component.

**Table_S20_concentration_metrics.** Annual rainfall-concentration metrics; definitions in Table S21.

**Table_S21_concentration_trends.** Trends in concentration metrics (definitions given), Hamed–Rao MK with block-bootstrap Sen CIs, BH q within family.

**Table_S22_frequency_intensity.** Relative Sen trends (% per decade, 95% block-bootstrap CI) of rain in wet pentads (≥5 mm), their number and their mean intensity, estimated on log scale. Log totals are additive, but separately estimated Sen slopes need not sum exactly.

**Table_S23_native_grid_quality_control.** Native CHIRPS grid: coverage, analysed cells and agreement of the grid-derived area-weighted pentad series with the archived Earth Engine regional series.

**Table_S24_pixel_trend_summary_CHIRPSv2.** Pixel-wise Hamed–Rao MK and Sen slopes (per decade) over 915 CHIRPS v2 cells (≥50% inside the boundary): area-weighted mean slope, 10th–90th percentile of slopes, and % of area with significant increase/decrease after BH-FDR (q ≤ 0.10, field significance; q ≤ 0.05).

**Table_S25_pixel_trend_summary_CHIRPSv3.** As Table S24 for CHIRPS v3 on the same grid.

**Table_S26_pixel_trends_v2_v3_agreement.** Agreement of pixel-wise trends between CHIRPS v2 and v3: mean slopes, % of cells with the same sign, spatial correlation of slopes, and % of area significant (q ≤ 0.10).

**Table_S27_moran_lisa_summary.** Global Moran's I of Sen-slope fields (permutation p) and area of significant Local Moran's I clusters (9999 conditional permutations, BH-FDR q ≤ 0.05); queen (primary) and rook (sensitivity) contiguity.

**Table_S28_pixel_values_CHIRPSv2.** Cell-level values: centre coordinates, coverage, area weight, Sen slope per decade, BH q and LISA class for each metric.

**Table_S29_zone_number_selection.** Spatially constrained Ward clustering of standardized annual-rainfall anomalies: silhouette, Calinski–Harabasz and Davies–Bouldin indices, smallest zone area and year-resampling stability (adjusted Rand index, 200 replicates).

**Table_S30_zone_trends.** Regional tests repeated for each exploratory zone (area-weighted zone means).

**Table_S31_zone_slope_contrasts.** Differences in Sen slopes between zones with joint moving-block bootstrap 95% CIs.
